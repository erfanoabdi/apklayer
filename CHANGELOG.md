### The C++ tool (cli/, 2026-10-28)
`bin/apklayer` execs `b/cli/apklayer` (build: `meson setup b && ninja -C b`, needs g++). Commands: `install|run|list|uninstall|stop|hub|component-host|env`, plus the test helpers `stack <lib> [args]` (services + one tool in the foreground, ends with the tool, tool output in `<logs>/tool.log`) and `exec <lib> [args]` (one library under the Android environment). Readiness is a system property of the instance, as on Android: the daemon sets `apklayer.stack.ready=1` once SurfaceFlinger (and the audio policy service) registered (`servicetool wait`), clients block in `aprop wait apklayer.stack.ready 1` on the property store's serial futex; no timers, no marker files. `scripts/native-env.sh` and `arm64-stack.sh` are gone. Open: multi-instance (P9) is deferred.
